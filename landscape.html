Landscape regrade

The landscape rebuilt to sit with the generated characters. Colour is not eyeballed: every albedo map in the generated set was decoded and k-means clustered in OKLab (scripts/tripo-palette.mjs), and the landscape palette is derived from those cluster centres. Drag any frame to wipe between before and after. Same seed, same camera, same lighting — only the terrain, materials and palette changed.

What the measurement said

SurfaceBeforeGenerated assets measuredAfterThe gap
Hue was the real mismatch, not saturation. The old greens sat at OKLab hue 135–147 — a cold, blue-leaning green — while every generated asset with foliage on it clustered at 107–129. The old earth ran 62–74 against a measured 37–71. Chroma only overshot on four of nineteen colours, so grading saturation alone would never have closed it.

The terrain stays a fully destructible heightfield. Nothing here is baked: occlusion is computed from the heights and recomputed on every carve, so a fresh crater shades its own bowl.
